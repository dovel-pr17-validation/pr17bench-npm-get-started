# Privacy policy

*Last updated: November 5, 2026*

The Personal Knowledge Library is built so that your notes stay yours. In short: **we collect nothing.**

## What the app stores, and where

Everything you create (notes, attachments, history, links, libraries,
notebooks, tags, settings, and saved searches) is stored **only in your
browser, on your device**, in its IndexedDB storage. Backup times and the
backup folder you choose are kept there too. None of it is sent to us or to
anyone else. There is no account, no server, and no sync service.

Your notes leave the device only when you move them yourself: by exporting a
backup or Markdown files, or by choosing a backup folder, which may be one a
cloud drive syncs. That copy is then governed by wherever you put it.

## What the app does not do

- No analytics, tracking, or advertising.
- No cookies.
- No error reports or crash reports sent anywhere. Errors are shown to you and,
  if you like, can be copied from Settings to include in a report you choose to
  send.
- No AI or third-party processing of your notes.

## Network use

The app's own files are downloaded from where it is hosted (GitHub Pages) when
you first open it and when it updates, and are then kept for offline use. The
handbooks and their pictures are among those files, so reading them fetches
nothing, and nobody learns what you read. If a note links to an image on the
web (`![…](https://…)`), it shows a **Load image** button instead; only when you
press it does your browser fetch the image, directly from that website.

## The host

The app is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log the
requests made for the app's files, including IP addresses, under the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
Those logs never contain your notes.

## Deleting your data

Delete notes in the app (and empty them from Trash), or remove everything by
clearing the site's data in your browser settings or uninstalling the app.
Backups and exports you saved elsewhere must be deleted where you saved them.

## Children

The Personal Knowledge Library collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

Changes to this policy are made in the open, in this file in the project's
repository, and noted in the changelog.
