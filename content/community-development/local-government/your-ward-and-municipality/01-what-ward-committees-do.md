Every municipality is divided into **wards**. Each ward elects a **ward councillor**, and each ward has a **ward committee**.

![A ward councillor listening to community members at a meeting outside the municipal offices.](01-what-ward-committees-do.svg)

## Who is on it

The ward committee is **chaired by the ward councillor**. Its members represent different parts of the ward and different interests, such as youth, women, business, health, education, and safety.

## What it does

A ward committee is a formal link between the community and the municipality. It:

- raises residents' concerns with the councillor and the municipality,
- helps represent the community's needs in the municipality's **Integrated Development Plan (IDP)** and **budget**,
- helps keep the community informed about municipal plans and decisions, and
- encourages residents to take part.

Ward committees **advise**; they do not make final decisions. Council does. But a strong, active ward committee makes sure the community's voice is heard.

## How you can take part

- Find out who your ward councillor and ward committee members are, from the municipality's office or website.
- Go to **ward meetings**, which should be announced in advance.
- Raise issues through your sector's ward committee member.
- Stand for the ward committee yourself when members are chosen.
