It is never too late to learn. Many adults go back to finish school, learn to read and write, or gain a skill for work.

![An adult learner arriving at a community college with books in hand.](01-going-back-to-learning.svg)

## Community Education and Training (CET) colleges

South Africa has nine public **CET colleges**, one in each province, with thousands of **Community Learning Centres** in schools, libraries, and community halls. They are for adults and young people who are out of school, and they offer:

- **Adult Education and Training (AET)**, from learning to read and write up to AET Level 4,
- the **General Education and Training Certificate (GETC)**, equal to Grade 9,
- in some centres, the **Senior Certificate for adults**, to finish matric, and
- skills programmes and short courses.

Ask at your nearest Community Learning Centre, or contact the CET college for your province, about what is offered near you, when classes run, and any costs.

## Other routes

- **TVET colleges** offer practical, work-focused courses after Grade 9 or matric.
- **Learnerships and apprenticeships** combine paid work with study; look out for them through employers and the sector education and training authorities (SETAs).
- **Free online courses** are available, if you have data or can use a library's internet.

## Getting started

1. Decide what you want: to read better, finish school, or learn a skill for work.
2. Visit a learning centre and ask what fits.
3. Start with one course. See [[Study habits that work]].
