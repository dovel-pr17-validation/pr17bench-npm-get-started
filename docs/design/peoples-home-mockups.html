Staying hydrated

A practical guide to building simple, steady hydration habits through the day.

About 5 min read   ·   Chapter 3 of 8

Water through the day

Hydration is part of everyday wellbeing. Small, regular choices can make it easier to drink enough, especially during warm weather or active days.

Keep water where it is easy to reach. A glass with meals and a refillable bottle nearby can help create a gentle routine without turning it into a task to track.

Needs vary from person to person. Follow advice from a qualified health professional for individual medical concerns.

Notice what works for you

Thirst, activity, food, and the weather all shape what your body needs. Build a routine that fits your day and the water available to you.
